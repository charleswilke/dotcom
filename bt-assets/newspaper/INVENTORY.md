# Northern Star clippings — inventory

University), byline **Chaz Wilke, Weekender Reporter**. Run spans **Sept. 4, 2003 –
Apr. 1, 2004** (Volume 104). Source photos are 4032×3024 or 5712×4284 — plenty of
resolution to transcribe from after rotating and cropping to columns.

`bt-assets/*` is excluded from the Vercel deploy, so these stay private. Cleaned
clipping scans that ship go to `images/before-times/archive/` as `.webp`.

## The recurring column

Most of the campus pieces run under a standing **NIU Review** rule — a graded review
of some piece of campus infrastructure (seats, bathrooms, carpet, wind, drinking
fountains, hot cocoa). That's the spine of the collection and the most distinctive
thing in it.

## Notes for whoever picks this up

- **Multi-page story.** "Meet Sam" spans four photos: IMG_4647 (cover reshoot),
  IMG_4622 (page 2), IMG_4623 (page 3), and the superseded IMG_4621. Reading order is
  cover → p2 left column → p3 left column → p2 right column → p3 right column; each
  seam was verified by matching the broken sentence across the jump.
- **IMG_4647 clips the headline.** The reshoot frame cuts the tops of the letters in
  "Meet Sam." It reads fine and is what ships today, but a reshoot framed ~1cm higher
  would make a cleaner hero image.
- **Orientation — read this before regenerating any scan.** `sips` is not reliable
  here and cost a full re-do of nine images. Three traps, all confirmed the hard way:
  1. **`sips -c` (crop) can rotate the output** relative to what `sips -Z` renders
     from the same file. Never compute crop coordinates from a `-Z` preview and
     assume they apply.
  2. **Rotations do not compose predictably.** `-r 90` then `-r 180` is not `-r 270`.
  3. **`sips -Z` alone preserves orientation correctly.** The safe recipe is
     `sips -Z 1500 <src>` → `cwebp`, adding a single `-r 90` only if needed. Avoid
     cropping unless a headline demands it; a little binder background looks fine.
- **ALWAYS verify the final `.webp`, not an intermediate.** Convert it back with
  `sips -s format jpeg -Z 400 out.webp --out check.jpg` and actually look at it.
  Checking the pre-crop file is what let nine broken images ship.
- **Watch zsh loops.** `for pair in "a b"; do set -- $pair; ...` does not split in
  this shell — both iterations silently processed the same file. Write the calls out.
- **Transcription method that worked:** rotate upright, then read the whole clipping at
  ~1600px. Most NIU Review columns are legible at that scale with no cropping. Only
  fall back to cropping individual columns at full resolution when the newsprint is
  dense or the photo is soft (IMG_4610 needed it; the four after it did not).
- **Photo credits.** Only **one** photo in the whole set is Chaz's own (IMG_4640,
  "CHAZ WILKE PHOTO"). Everything else is a Northern Star staff photographer
  (Waynauskas, Staples, Kapff, Swedberg, Klimek, Lattman, Iwabuchi), a "Star file
  photo," or a wire/studio still (movieweb.com, Columbia, KRTdirect, "courtesy").
  The settled approach: ship the **whole clipping scan** as the artifact, with the
  printed credit line intact and visible, rather than extracting the photo and
  republishing it standalone. That reads as a preserved document rather than as
  reuse of someone else's image — and it looks better in the dispenser anyway.
  Every entry also names the photographer in the transcription's closing line.
  (Correction: the credit on IMG_4639 reads **Scott Lattyak**, not Lattman.)
- **IMG_4644 is held out of the archive — attribution confirmed.** The "greetings from
  DeKalb … it really sucks here!!!" postcard was drawn by **John Ugolini**, the Northern
  Star's staff cartoonist (confirmed by Chaz, July 2026). The scrap carries no printed
  byline. It stays out: this is his artwork cut out on its own, not a clipping of a
  Chaz piece that happens to carry art, so the whole-clipping rule doesn't cover it.
  Ugolini also appears *inside* the archive as a named source — he supplies the
  Beelzebub quote in "In the name of all things ghostly."
  To ship it, get his permission and credit him on the entry.
- **IMG_4618 pulled from the archive (Chaz's call, July 2026).** "A visit from Satan's
  little minions" closes by renaming the Asian lady beetles "Bin Laden bugs" and
  calling for a national bug-whacking day to "unite America against yet another
  imaginary foe." It is an October 2003 artifact and it reads very differently now.
  The entry and its `.webp` were removed; the source photo stays in this folder, so
  it is reversible. If it is ever restored, the closing four paragraphs are the issue
  — the radon-hallucination premise ahead of them is the good part and stands alone.
- **Undated clippings.** Seven were cut without their masthead, so their issue dates
  are unrecoverable from the scrap: the two NIU Review columns IMG_4639 and IMG_4642,
  and the five reviews IMG_4609 / IMG_4612 / IMG_4615 / IMG_4617 / IMG_4619. Their
  entries say so rather than guessing.

## Where it lives in the site

- Collection: `press: [...]` in [before-times-archive.js](../../before-times-archive.js),
  alongside `content` and `alchemy`. The archive renderer is generic over collection
  name, so entries need no new plumbing.
- Panel copy + button: `PANELS.press` in [before-times.js](../../before-times.js).
  Button action `'clippings'` opens `openArchive('press')`, which lands on the
  first entry in the collection.
- Header text per collection: `ARCHIVE_HEADERS` in before-times.js.
- Images: `images/before-times/archive/<slug>-v1.webp`, ~1400px on the long edge.
