# From This Side of the Door — conversation archive

A working session from October 24, 2026 between Charles Wilke and Codex behind “From This Side of the Door.” Beginning with the essays “Communion” and “Vulnerable,” the conversation follows the first lyric, a heartland-rock arrangement, Charles’s edits, a discussion of singability, and the shift toward dark chamber folk with a slow soul groove. All 16 creative-session turns are preserved, including the early drafts, both 1,000-character style prompts, and the final lyric with production cues. Routine progress updates, tool output, internal instructions and reasoning, empty editing notifications, and requests about packaging this transcript are omitted. Writing-block wrappers are removed, lyric line breaks are preserved, and chapter headings are editorial additions.

Public reader after deployment: https://charleswilke.com/read/from-this-side-of-the-door-transcript

This uses the same transcript reader, speaker labels, chapter headings, and download structure as the Substack companion conversations. The HTML fragment is `transcripts/from-this-side-of-the-door-transcript.html`; its entry in `LOCAL_ARTICLES` supplies the reader title and description. The existing article build generates its permanent `/read/` page data and sitemap entry.

- `conversation.md`: portable transcript, with every creative-session turn.
- `conversation.json`: the same turns as structured source text, plus chapter boundaries.
- `assets/charles-edited-lyrics.txt`: Charles’s pasted lyric before the singability revisions.
- `assets/final-lyrics-with-production-cues.txt`: the accepted lyric with the final chamber-folk production cues.
- `assets/style-heartland-rock.txt`: the original 1,000-character style prompt.
- `assets/style-chamber-folk.txt`: the final 1,000-character style prompt, exactly 1,000 characters with no trailing newline.
- `manifest.json`: permanent reader URL, source essays, turn count, and asset checksums.
- `from-this-side-of-the-door-conversation.zip`: portable package of these files and the reader fragment.
