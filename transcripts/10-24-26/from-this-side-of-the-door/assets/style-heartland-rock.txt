Soulful heartland rock, 84 BPM, 4/4, intimate storytelling that opens into a warm, full band. A weathered, expressive lead vocal: conversational verses, a little rasp, dry humor, breath and human imperfections. Begin with close acoustic guitar and natural room sound. First chorus adds round bass and sparse piano; let the melody rise on 'fine' and soften into the admission about footsteps. Bring in a relaxed backbeat for verse two, then warm Hammond organ and short, clean electric guitar replies. Keep the groove patient and gently moving. Pull back to voice and guitar at the bridge, building beneath 'Got a minute? Listen to this.' Open the final chorus with full drums, ringing electric guitar, piano and a rough harmony on the hook. More chest and conviction in the vocal, with the lift of finally letting someone hear you. Keep the feeling affectionate, earthy, soulful and lightly scrappy.
