HELP YOURSELF — DIRTY ROOM PASS
Inspired by Artifacts, Charles Wilke

STYLE (979 characters)

Raw piano-led art rock, lurching post-punk pulse, 86 BPM. Detuned upright piano hammered into a crooked repeating figure; rattling strings, uneven hammers, hard low notes. Overdriven electric bass with pick clank and a stubborn two-note throb. Boxy kick, loose snare wires, floor-tom thumps, scrappy hi-hat; drums drag against the bass. Electric guitar spits dissonant stabs, dirty tremolo and brief feedback tails. Small band recorded live in one cramped room: mic bleed, overloaded preamps, tape compression, amp hum. Solo low male baritone, rough and intelligible, half-spoken with bitten consonants, dry chuckles and cracked sung phrases. A merry ghost enjoys its own estate sale, offering unheard asides. Wry, affectionate delivery. Refrains get more insistent; band remains unruly. Needlepoint verse drops to rattling piano and voice. No country twang, folk-pop, crooning, growling, choir, lush strings or glossy crescendo. End on a blunt piano chord with amp buzz hanging.
